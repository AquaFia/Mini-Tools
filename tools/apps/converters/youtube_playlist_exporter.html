YouTube Playlist Exporter

Export an entire YouTube playlist without copying tracks one by one. The bookmarklet runs directly on the playlist page, scrolls to load the entries, then copies a tab-separated list and downloads a CSV.

1
Show your bookmarks bar.
Chrome/Edge: Ctrl+Shift+B. Safari: View → Show Favorites Bar.
2
Drag the red button below onto your bookmarks bar.
3
Open a YouTube playlist, click the bookmarklet, and let it finish loading all tracks.
▶ YouTube Playlist Exporter

Output columns: #, Title, Channel, URL. The tool also copies the same information to your clipboard so you can paste it straight into ChatGPT.

Notes / troubleshooting
• Run it from a normal YouTube playlist page.
• Very large playlists may take a little longer because YouTube only loads entries as you scroll.
• If YouTube changes its page structure in the future, the selectors may need updating.
• The export happens locally in your browser; this HTML does not upload your playlist anywhere.
